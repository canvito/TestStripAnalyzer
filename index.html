How to read these results
  • Value is always an estimate, interpolated between the two closest chart colors; the range in brackets covers every value whose chart color is practically indistinguishable (within 2 ΔE₀₀) from your pad.
  • ≈ value, "above/below chart": the pad's color goes past the chart's last color, so the value is extrapolated along the chart's color trend (up to one chart step beyond). > value means it is even further out.
  • Status (Normal / Low / High, or Very low / Very high when it needs action now) compares the estimate with the target range of the Tank type chosen at the top; readings outside the target show their Potential impact and a Suggested mitigation in the overview table, with full steps under What to do.
  • Color match tells how close the pad is to the chart's color path: good ≤ 3 ΔE, fair ≤ 6, poor > 6 (check glare, shadows, marker position and timing).
  • The bar shows the chart colors as captured in your photo; ▲ marks the reading.
  • KH & GH should be read immediately, pH/NO₂/NO₃ after 30 s. With the timer running, each photo is tagged and the best-timed photo is used per parameter.
